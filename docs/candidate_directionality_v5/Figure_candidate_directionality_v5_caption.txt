Figure 2. Climatic associations and observed candidate-locus geography in Ameiva ameiva. (a) Mean signed LFMM2 effects for the 164 SNPs shared by the final partial-RDA and LFMM2 candidate sets, grouped by the predictor with the largest absolute LFMM2 coefficient among each SNP's original q ≤ 0.05 associations and by coefficient sign. Row labels give SNPs/distinct RAD loci. The heatmap shows all four predictor coefficients for every group, not just the coefficient used for grouping. Effects are changes in standardized VCF ALT dosage per one standard deviation of climate; purple indicates negative, orange positive, and white approximately zero. The coefficient object was recovered by refitting the original LFMM2 specification with effect-size output and reproducing its z scores and P values to numerical precision. (b) Observed mean ALT frequency at 185 coordinate-defined localities across positive and negative groups for BIO2 (diurnal temperature range, °C), BIO3 (isothermality, ratio), BIO13 (wettest-month precipitation, mm), and BIO14 (driest-month precipitation, mm). Small points are locality observations. Larger points and connecting lines are individual-count-weighted descriptive means of ten observed climate-quantile bins, not fitted LFMM2 relationships or conditional effects. Frequencies are mean unflipped ALT dosage divided by two across individuals and SNPs within each group. (c) Observed locality scores for BIO14+ and BIO13−, chosen before plotting as the two largest groups with at least 25 SNPs and positive climate-added held-out RMSE improvement in all four existing checkerboard folds. Each score is the locality mean of SNP-wise standardized ALT dosage, standardized again across the 185 localities within its group. The common zero-centred display scale is saturated beyond ±2.815 within-group locality SD (the symmetric 2nd–98th-percentile limit across the eight-group map set). Point size denotes sampled individuals per locality; no genomic scores are interpolated into unsampled areas. (d) Mean cumulative Gradient Forest (GF) split importance along observed climate ECDFs for BIO7, BIO2, and BIO4 from the controlled full-panel model fitted to 14,134 SNPs and ten climatic predictors. The 32 batches were computational partitions, not biological replicates; curve colours distinguish predictors and do not indicate ALT-association direction. This GF model is distinct from the 164 shared linear candidates in panels a–c. ALT is a reference-relative allele designation, not a beneficial allele, and neither LFMM2 associations nor GF importance establish fitness effects.

# Candidate directionality v4: scientific audit

I built the v4 figure from the permanent development directory. I preserved all v2 and v3 scripts, tables and figures and did not refit the genome-wide Gradient Forest, redo candidate selection, or change the manuscript.

## Source and effect lineage

I verified that the intersection of the saved final RDA consensus (889 SNPs) and LFMM2 FDR05 set (314 SNPs) contains exactly the 164 identifiers in the signed-group table. The imputed matrix has 245 InternalID-aligned individuals, 14,134 uniquely named SNP columns, finite dosages limited to 0, 1 and 2, and no missing values. I retained VCF ALT coding throughout; no frequency was silently reversed. The original LFMM2 fits standardized one climate predictor at a time with K=4 latent factors but did not retain coefficient objects. The previous effect-size-enabled LEA 3.22.0 reconstruction reproduced their saved z scores, P values and genomic-inflation factors to maximum absolute differences of 9.12e-11, 4.27e-11 and 2.68e-11, with coefficient/z-score sign agreement for all 14,134 SNPs in each fit. This is a numerically verified reconstruction, not a direct read of an original saved fit.

## Eight groups and RAD linkage

The counts are BIO2+ 10 SNPs/8 RAD loci, BIO2- 30/26, BIO3+ 8/8, BIO3- 7/5, BIO13+ 12/11, BIO13- 34/32, BIO14+ 37/32 and BIO14- 26/22. Overall there are 143 distinct RAD loci; the sum of per-group RAD counts is 144 because a RAD locus can occur in more than one signed group. The one-SNP-per-RAD sensitivity used the first existing candidate identifier within each group and changed no sign of the eight adjusted slopes. Original versus pruned individual group-frequency correlations range from 0.976 to 1.000. The prior training-only LFMM2 refits across the original four checkerboard folds retained mean signed-group assignment at 92.9-100% depending on group. Candidate identities and original q <= 0.05 predictor sets were held fixed during these refits; they are sensitivity checks rather than independent candidate rediscovery.

## Observed and adjusted directions

For each group I averaged imputed ALT dosage/2 across its SNPs within each individual and then averaged across sampled individuals at identical coordinates. Thus the frequencies are for observed sampling sites but are derived from the validated imputed genotype matrix, not complete raw genotype calls. This makes each SNP contribute equally; all sites have their observed sample size in the locality table. The main figure connects descriptive, individual-count-weighted locality means within climate deciles. It never treats those lines as LFMM2 fit curves. The conditional diagnostic instead uses an individual-level quasi-binomial fractional-response GLM with a logit link, one weight per individual, and genomic PC1-PC3 plus longitude and latitude as nuisance terms. It predicts frequencies within zero and one and does not pretend that linked SNPs are independent binomial trials. Climate is standardized on the training set separately within each spatial fold.

All eight full-data conditional slopes and all 32 training-fold slopes have the original LFMM2 sign. The marginal locality slope disagrees in sign for BIO13+ (near zero and negative marginally, positive conditionally) and BIO14- (weakly positive marginally, negative conditionally). These discrepancies are important evidence that observed geography and conditional association answer different questions. Climate improves held-out RMSE in all four folds for BIO2+, BIO2-, BIO3-, BIO13-, BIO14+ and BIO14-. BIO3+ and BIO13+ improve in three of four folds. The globally defined candidate panel and genome-wide PCs make this a spatial sensitivity analysis, not independent proof of selection or fully external prediction.

## Figure decisions and limitations

Panel A adds both SNP and RAD counts. Panel B shows all four climates and both signs using observed localities and bounded descriptive decile means. Panel C shows the two largest groups with at least 25 SNPs and four positive held-out climate contributions (BIO14+ and BIO13-); all eight maps are retained in the supplement. Panel D preserves the controlled 14,134-SNP GF curves and has no false batch-SD uncertainty ribbon. The eight map scores are standardized within group across localities for display, with a common zero-centred +/-2.815 robust limit; 29 of 1,480 locality-group points are colour-saturated. Neither the map scores nor GF cumulative importance imply direction of selection or fitness. Candidate PC1 remains strongly correlated with background genomic PC1 (rho = 0.907), and many localities contain just one individual. The small BIO3 groups and the weak/inconsistent BIO13+ spatial gain need restrained interpretation. The baseline PCs were estimated from all individuals, and candidate discovery preceded fold testing; these remain sources of information reuse.

I used R 4.4.0, ggplot2 4.0.3 and scales 1.4.0 for v4; the earlier LFMM2 reconstruction used LEA 3.22.0. I did not infer missing climate, genome-annotation or GF model metadata from the plotted patterns.

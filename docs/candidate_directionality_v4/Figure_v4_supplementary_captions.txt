Supplementary Figure Sx. Complete observed frequency profiles for the eight signed LFMM2 response groups. Positive and negative groups are shown separately for BIO2, BIO3, BIO13 and BIO14 in their original CHELSA units (degrees Celsius, dimensionless ratio and mm, respectively). Faint points represent the mean unflipped VCF ALT allele frequency across SNPs and sampled individuals at each of 185 coordinate-defined localities; point size reflects the number of individuals. Solid points and joining lines are descriptive, individual-count-weighted means of localities in ten climate-quantile bins. They are not fitted LFMM2 functions or structure-adjusted trends. Sparse observations at climatic extremes can make adjacent bin means change sharply; the complete locality table retains the underlying data and sample sizes.

Supplementary Figure Sy. Observed geography of all eight signed LFMM2 response groups. For each group, SNP-wise standardized ALT dosages were averaged by individual and then by locality; the 185 locality means were standardized within the group for display. Purple and orange show lower and higher scores relative to that group's sampled-locality mean, not lower or higher adaptation. All panels share the same extent and symmetric zero-centred display scale, saturated outside +/-2.815 within-group SD. Point size represents sampled individuals. Only observed locality coordinates are plotted; no genomic surface was interpolated.

Supplementary Figure Sz. Spatial holdout sensitivity of group-level climate terms. Each point is one of the four existing checkerboard folds. The x axis is the held-out RMSE of a quasi-binomial fractional-response model with genomic PC1-PC3, longitude and latitude, minus the held-out RMSE of the same model with the group's focal climate predictor added. Positive values favour the climate-added model. Climate was standardized using training individuals only; predictions are bounded between zero and one. Colour indicates whether the training climate slope retained the group's original LFMM2 sign. The 164 candidate SNPs and genome-wide PCs were defined using the complete dataset before this sensitivity analysis, so these are not independent tests of candidate selection or fully external predictions.

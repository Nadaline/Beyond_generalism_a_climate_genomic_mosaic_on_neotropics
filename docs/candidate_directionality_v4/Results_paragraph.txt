Among the 164 shared partial-RDA/LFMM2 candidates, the largest signed groups were BIO14+ (37 SNPs, 32 RAD loci), BIO13- (34, 32), BIO2- (30, 26) and BIO14- (26, 22); the remaining groups contained 7-12 SNPs each. Observed ALT frequencies varied across the sampled gradients of diurnal temperature range, isothermality, and wettest- and driest-month precipitation, but the marginal patterns did not always mirror conditional associations: BIO13+ had an almost flat, slightly negative marginal slope despite a positive structure- and geography-adjusted slope, whereas BIO14- had a slightly positive marginal slope despite a negative adjusted slope. Adjusted slopes retained the LFMM2 sign for all eight groups, as did training estimates in each of the four spatial folds. Adding climate reduced held-out prediction error in all four folds for six groups; the weaker BIO13+ and BIO3+ groups improved in three of four. One-SNP-per-RAD analyses preserved all eight conditional directions. The two largest groups with consistent held-out improvement mapped contrasting observed western/northern and eastern locality scores, but the candidate-panel PC1 strongly tracked genome-wide PC1 (Spearman rho = 0.907), limiting interpretation of this geography independently of population history. Separately, the controlled full-panel Gradient Forest described cumulative climatic turnover across all 14,134 SNPs, not allele-frequency direction in the 164 linear candidates.

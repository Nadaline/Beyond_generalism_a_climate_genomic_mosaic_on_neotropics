Figure X. Climatic response groups and their observed geography in Ameiva ameiva. (a) Mean signed LFMM2 effects for all 164 SNPs shared by the final partial-RDA and LFMM2 candidate sets. Each SNP was assigned to the predictor with the largest absolute LFMM2 coefficient among its original q <= 0.05 associations; the plus/minus sign refers to the slope of VCF ALT dosage, not to fitness, ancestry or derived state. Labels give SNPs/distinct RAD loci. All four fitted predictor coefficients are shown for each group, including coefficients that did not independently determine its assignment. Coefficients are ALT-dosage change per one standard deviation of climate divided by that SNP's ALT-dosage standard deviation before averaging. Purple is negative, orange positive, and white zero. The original LFMM2 run did not save its coefficient object; the displayed values were recovered by refitting its specification with effect-size output enabled and reproducing the original z scores and P values to numerical precision. (b) Observed mean ALT allele frequency at 185 coordinate-defined localities for positive and negative groups of BIO2 (mean diurnal temperature range, degrees Celsius), BIO3 (isothermality, ratio), BIO13 (wettest-month precipitation, mm), and BIO14 (driest-month precipitation, mm). Faint points are localities; coloured points and connecting lines are descriptive means in ten observed climate-quantile bins, weighted by the number of sampled individuals per locality. Frequencies are the mean unflipped ALT dosage divided by two across individuals and SNPs in a group; lines are not fitted LFMM2 or conditional responses. (c) Observed locality maps for BIO14+ and BIO13-, selected before graphical inspection as the two largest groups with at least 25 SNPs and positive climate-added held-out RMSE improvement in all four existing checkerboard folds. Each map displays the locality mean of SNP-wise standardized ALT dosages, further standardized across the 185 localities within that group. A shared, zero-centred purple-white-orange colour scale is saturated outside +/-2.815 within-group locality SD, based on the 2nd and 98th percentiles across the eight-group map set. No values are predicted between sampled sites. (d) Mean cumulative Gradient Forest split importance along observed ECDF-scaled climatic gradients for BIO7, BIO2, and BIO4, from the controlled model fitted to all 14,134 SNPs and ten climate predictors. The 32 SNP batches were computational partitions, not population replicates; their variation is not presented as uncertainty. Panel d is separate from the 164 linear candidates, and its cumulative importance is not directional allele-frequency change. All panels are descriptive and do not identify a beneficial ALT allele or establish fitness consequences.
